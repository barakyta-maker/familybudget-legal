Delete your Family Budget cloud account and data

App: Family Budget
Developer: Polvarix
Support: kvozipol@gmail.com

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Family Budget.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Under Support & information, open Cloud account.
  4. Select Delete cloud account and data.
  5. Read the warning and confirm permanent deletion.

What is deleted

The deletion request removes the authenticated anonymous Supabase Auth account and cloud data associated with that account. If the user owns a cloud family, the owned cloud family and its synchronized family records are removed. If the user is a member of another family, that membership and family records authored by that member are removed.

What is not deleted by this action

Your local budget database on the phone is not automatically deleted by the cloud-account deletion action. This prevents a cloud-account request from unexpectedly destroying local-only budget records.

Google Play subscription

Deleting the Family Budget cloud account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. Subscription management and cancellation are handled separately by Google Play.

If you cannot use the app

Contact kvozipol@gmail.com and request deletion of your Family Budget cloud account/data. Do not send passwords, purchase tokens, or financial transaction details by email.

Timing

In-app deletion is designed to execute immediately when the server is reachable. If a deletion request cannot be completed, the app reports an error and the user can retry or contact support.
