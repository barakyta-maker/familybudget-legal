What family sharing means

When family synchronization is enabled, information marked as family data can be visible to invited members of the same Family Budget family. Personal transactions remain local to the user's device.

Rules

Do not use member names, categories, merchant/note fields or other family-shared fields for threats, harassment, hateful or degrading content, sexual content, illegal content, spam, fraud or other abusive material.

Family sharing is intended only for legitimate household/family budgeting and coordination.

Reporting and blocking

Connected members can report another connected member from the Family screen. A family owner can report and block a member. A non-owner member can report abuse and leave the family immediately.

Safety reports may contain family/member identifiers, the selected report reason, optional details supplied by the reporter and the safety action taken.

Enforcement

Blocked members cannot silently reconnect to the same family member slot. Removed or disconnected members require a new valid invitation to reconnect, where permitted.
